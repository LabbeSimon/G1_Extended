## Extensions are your call

Extensions are declarative content — cards for the lens — never executable
code, and the app validates them against fixed limits before installing.
Even so: **you are responsible for what you install.** Read what an
extension is, who wrote it, and which web addresses it reads from — the app
shows you all of it before the install button.

A lens mirror in Settings > Debug: 576x136 at one bit per pixel, drawn from
the bytes actually written to the glasses, so a layout can be checked without
wearing anything. It reports what runs off the panel, what leaves the text
column, and how much of the world the display costs.

The lens can be exported as a PNG, and the line width the glasses turn out to
want can be kept without waiting for a release.

Text lines are cut at the width the lens really has rather than half of it.
The dashboard's second pane accepts news cards and a map image for the first
time. Events the glasses send are named and listed instead of discarded.
